# Google Drive Layout

The current top level is deliberately short:

- `Archive & Backups`
- `Automotive & Shop`
- `Education`
- `Homelab`
- `LyfeOS`
- `Media`
- `Personal`
- `Projects`
- `Recipes`

Deep filing follows ownership and purpose:

- Issued personal records: `Personal/Licenses/EPA 608`
- Resumes: `Personal/Resumes`
- WGU course work: `Education/WGU/<course code and name>`
- E010 drills: `Education/WGU/E010 Foundations of Programming in Python/Phone Drills`
- CompTIA material: `Education/Certifications/CompTIA/<A+, Network+, Security+, Cloud+, or Tech+>`
- CCNA Boson material: `Education/Certifications/Cisco/CCNA/Boson Labs`
- CCNA commands: `Education/Certifications/Cisco/CCNA/Phone & Command Drills`
- LPI material: `Education/Certifications/LPI/Linux Essentials`
- ITIL material: `Education/Certifications/PeopleCert/ITIL 4 Foundation`
- Course-specific completed work: `Education/WGU/Completed Courses/<course code and name>`
- Recipe search hub: `Recipes/Recipe Library`
- Raw recipe sources: the narrow Food, Mushroom Cultivation, Laundry, or Templates folder under `Recipes`

Automotive uses one hub per vehicle:

- `Automotive & Shop/Vehicles/2015 Subaru WRX VA/{Manuals & Reference, Maintenance & Parts, Receipts & Purchases}`
- `Automotive & Shop/Vehicles/2025 Honda Civic Type R FL5/{Manuals & Reference, Maintenance & Parts, Receipts & Purchases}`
- `Automotive & Shop/Vehicles/2000 Mazda Miata NB/{Manuals & Reference, Maintenance & Parts, Receipts & Purchases}`
- `Automotive & Shop/Shop Tools & Supplies/Garage Tools`

Canonical receipt evidence remains under `LyfeOS/02 Receipts & Purchases/Receipt Archive/02 Receipts by Category`. Vehicle and tool hubs contain links to those canonical records, not duplicate financial copies. Multi-vehicle orders have one canonical receipt plus explicit vehicle references.

The old full-text receipt Doc lives under receipt backups and is never the front end. The 2015 WRX factory service manual and 2022–2024 Civic/FL5 reference manual live inside their exact vehicle `Manuals & Reference` folders.

An issued credential is personal; study material used to earn a credential is education. A filename is not trusted when its content contradicts it. Blank files are named by type and date and retained under `Archive & Backups/Blank Files` rather than left loose.
